# **4. Our Agency & Trade Facilitation Model** 

We believe successful trade depends on connecting the right businesses, establishing trust, and maintaining clear communication throughout the transaction. 

LEAD MULTI-CHOICE ® operates as a local facilitator and business representation partner, helping international suppliers connect with relevant purchasing entities in Rwanda. 

Our model focuses on five key areas: 

1. **Connect:** Identify suitable buyers, suppliers, and market opportunities. 

2. **Facilitate:** Support introductions, commercial communication, and procurement engagement. 

3. **Coordinate:** Help align stakeholders, documentation, logistics, and delivery processes. 

4. **Monitor:** Follow up on agreed milestones, delivery progress, and outstanding operational matters. 

5. **Sustain:** Support after-sales relationships, warranty coordination, and future business opportunities. 

The buyer and seller remain the contracting parties and manage their commercial and payment obligations directly, unless otherwise agreed. Our role, authority, responsibilities, and any applicable agency fees or commissions are defined by the relevant engagement or agency agreement. 

This approach enables us to create value without unnecessarily duplicating the commercial responsibilities of the buyer or seller.
